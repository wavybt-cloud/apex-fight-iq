Inside the Model

Every trick in the engine, stated plainly. If you can't explain an edge, you don't have one.

01Power-rating core

Each team carries an offensive and defensive rating in points vs. league average. Projected margin = rating gap + home field + situational adjustments. Win probability comes from the margin over a normal distribution with the empirical NFL sigma (~13.2 pts regular season).

02Preseason engine

Starters barely play in August, so base ratings are compressed to 30% and a depth/motivation score takes over: roster-battle intensity, new head coaches proving systems, QB competitions, and veteran teams mailing it in. Home field drops to ~1 point, totals baseline drops to the preseason scoring environment, and sigma tightens because games are lower scoring.

03Situational layer

Rest differential (bye weeks, short weeks off Thursday), travel across time zones with a west-coast-body-clock penalty for early kicks, divisional familiarity shrinking spreads, and QB status — the single biggest line-mover in football, adjustable per game from starter to third string.

04No-vig market comparison

Market prices are de-vigged before comparison so the model is judged against the book's true implied probability, not the taxed one. Edges are quoted in points (spread/total) and probability (moneyline).

05Key numbers

NFL margins cluster on 3 and 7. The model flags any recommendation where the market line sits on or crosses a key number — that half point is worth real cents, and buying through 3 is often justified when the model agrees.

06Kelly staking

Stake = fractional Kelly from model probability vs. the actual price, capped by your max bet. Quarter Kelly by default — full Kelly is for people who enjoy ruin. Preseason stakes are halved on top of that.

07Self-tuning

Every graded game feeds an Elo-style margin update back into team ratings (reduced K in preseason, where signal is noisy), and graded win probabilities feed a Platt recalibration so the model's confidence stays honest.

08CLV — the real scoreboard

Closing Line Value is the only reliable evidence you're beating the market. Log the closing line on every pick; the dashboard tracks how often you beat the close and by how much. Beat the close consistently and profit follows. Don't, and no track record will save you.

Mark result
Apex AI NFL is a quantitative estimate engine, not a crystal ball. No model guarantees profit against closing NFL lines — the market is the strongest public prediction machine on earth. The honest goal is positive CLV and disciplined staking. Ratings are editable priors that self-tune from results; verify injuries and lines with your book before betting. 21+, bet responsibly.
